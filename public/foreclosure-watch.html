How to read this

Values

Estimated market value is the Property Appraiser's 2026 just value divided by 0.85. Florida appraisers deduct about 15% for costs of sale, so this reverses that step. It is a rough guide, not an appraisal. Check recent sales or Zillow before bidding.

Lien read
  • Equity: circuit-court case with a judgment between 15% and 100% of value. Usually a first-mortgage foreclosure, which wipes out junior liens.
  • HOA / junior lien: county-court cases, or tiny judgments. The buyer usually takes the property still subject to the first mortgage.
  • Underwater: judgment is at or above value. The bank usually keeps these.
Before you bid
  • Pull a title search: the case type is only a guess at which lien is being foreclosed.
  • Many sales get cancelled the day before (bankruptcy, payoff, reset). Broward often loses a third or more.
  • Competition pushes winning bids well above the judgment on strong-equity homes. The spread shown is the ceiling, not your profit.
  • Sales are as-is, usually occupied, with no interior access. Budget for eviction, repairs and unpaid HOA dues.
  • Florida law requires a 5% deposit, with the balance due by the next business day. Check the clerk's exact deadlines before bidding.
